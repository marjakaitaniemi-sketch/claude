Fun Facts About Russian Blues

  • Their coat has a unique double layer that shimmers in the light.
  • Russian Blues are known to be hypoallergenic — great for sensitive noses!
  • They are very quiet cats with soft, gentle voices.
  • They remember routines and will remind you when it's dinner time.
  • Russian Blues can live up to 15–20 years with love and care.
  • They tend to pick one special person as their very best friend.
